**HP:** 5

**STATS:** **S** +1, **D** +2, **C** +1, **I** +0, **W** +0, **Ch** +0  
**ALIGNMENT:** C  
**LEVEL:** 1

**Rubbery.** Half damage from stabbing weapons.

**HP:** 15  
**ATTACK:** 2 tendril +3 (1d6)

### MARROW FIEND

*A wolfish hulk of razor bone and sinewy muscle. Black marrow tree sap drips from its jaws and the gaps in its bony plating.*

**AC:** 15  
**HP:** 39  
**ATTACK:** 2 claws +7 (1d10) and sap gout (near line) +5 (2d6 + sap)  
**MOVE:** near (climb)  
**STATS:** **S** +4, **D** +4, **C** +3, **I** +2, **W** +3, **Ch** +3  
**ALIGNMENT:** C  
**LEVEL:** 8

**Devour.** Use turn to devour a humanoid body to regain 3d8 HP.

**Sap.** DC 15 DEX check or stuck in place. Repeat check on turn to escape.

### MUGDULBLUB

*A roiling mass of stars floating in a whirlpool of purple goo.*

**AC:** 16  
**HP:** 58  
**ATTACK:** 3 tendril (near) +8 (2d8) and dissolve  
**MOVE:** near (climb, swim)  
**STATS:** **S** +5, **D** +3, **C** +4, **I** +2, **W** +3, **Ch** +4  
**ALIGNMENT:** C  
**LEVEL:** 12

**Rubbery.** Half damage from stabbing weapons.

**Mutagenic.** Creatures within near 1:6 chance on their turn of a mutation. 1d4: **1.** Extra eye, **2-3.** Lost bone, **4.** Extra finger

**Dissolve.** One creature within far DC 15 CON or lose 1d4 CON. If reduced to 0, become ichor ooze under Mugdulblubʼs control.
