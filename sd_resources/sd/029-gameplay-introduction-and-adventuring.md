## ADVENTURING {#p77}

### CRAWLERS

Those who dare the lost halls and caverns of the Shadowdark are called by many names. Crawlers, delvers, explorers, adventurers, treasure hunters.

In this chapter, youʼll learn how to crawl with the best of them. Whether youʼre exploring a lightless barrow mound or the Shrouded Forest of Eld, the rules herein will guide you.

### OUTSIDE THE SHEET

The first step to crawling is understanding your environment and imagining an action you want to take.

Donʼt simply scour your character sheet for ideas and options! Your crawling career depends on your ability to “think outside the sheet.”

Imagine what you would really do if you were in your characterʼs situation.

Do you see a suit of armor in an alcove? Maybe you lift the helmʼs vizor and make sure nothing is looking back at you.

Do you hear faint voices behind a rotting, wooden door? Maybe you creep up and press your ear against it to hear whatʼs happening on the other side.

Is a band of orcs chasing you and your companions down a hallway? Maybe tearing the decrepit tapestries from the walls will hamper their pursuit.

None of the above ideas are listed on a character sheet. Although you should use your stats, spells, gear, and talents, donʼt limit yourself only to those.
