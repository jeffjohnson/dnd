## THE GLOAMING HEX MAP

![The Gloaming Hex Map]assets/cs1-gloaming-hex-map-p2.png)

### MAP DESCRIPTION

The Gloaming is a broad region of woodland, lakes, winding waterways, and open lowlands. Dense stands of trees cover much of the north, west, and east. The southern portion has more open ground, particularly southeast of the central lake, where marshy terrain and smaller bodies of water are depicted. Faint, winding paths cross the woodland and open terrain, linking parts of the region. The map depicts scattered buildings, ruins, camps, and other landmarks throughout the hexes.

**Format:** A flat-top hex grid arranged in staggered north–south columns. Columns are numbered west to east, and rows are numbered north to south. A hex identifier combines its column number (in hundreds) with its two-digit row number. For example, 1108 identifies column 11, row 08. Even-numbered columns contain 10 hexes; odd-numbered columns contain 11.

**Scale:** 1 hex = 2 miles.

**Keyed locations:** See The Gloaming Hex Key (pg. 33) for descriptions of numbered hex locations.

#### SIGNIFICANT LANDMARKS

**Finimere Lake** occupies the center-west of the map. Winding waterways run through the surrounding woodland. Two separate waterways empty into the lake with an outflow channel flowing out of the southeastern shore. The water continues flowing southeast towards the marsh with a bridge that sits on the edge where hexes 1008 and 1109 meet, connecting Marinʼs Hold with the western road.

**Toad Pond** lies in the northeastern corner of the region. Additional small pools and wet ground appear in the south and southeast.

**Added trail (not shown on the printed map):** An old woodland path connects hexes 1108 and 1308. Curious children use it when daring each other to visit the Myre Castle ruins (hex 1308).

#### TERRAIN AND FEATURES BY HEX

**Default terrain:** Forest, unless another terrain type is specified.

**Interpretation:** Paths and waterways may cross a hex without replacing its underlying terrain. Keyed location descriptions take precedence over this map-derived classification. A hex listed under multiple terrain types contains both; for example, a hex listed under Marsh and Water contains marshland and open water.

## A MINIATURE HEXCRAWL FEATURING THE GLOAMING

An ancient forest where black trees tower over mossy standing stones and mist-addled bogs. Here, fragile bastions of mankind cluster behind torchlit palisades and pray for deliverance from the demons that hatch from the forestʼs cursed **marrow trees**. The **Knights of St. Ydris** range out of their deepwood conclave to wage a losing war against these demons and their servants.

The knightsʼ leader, **Inquisitor Justinia Morvin**, suspects a traitor in their ranks.

## INTRODUCTION

Welcome to the first issue of *Cursed Scroll*!

Everything in this zine is ready to use with the free public ***Shadowdark RPG*** rules. You can check out the fully playable quickstart PDFs at [*thearcanelibrary.com/pages/shadowdark*](https://www.thearcanelibrary.com/pages/shadowdark).

This issueʼs theme is **diablerie**, and the pages inside are brimming with player and Game Master material about demons, deviltry, curses, the occult, and witchcraft.

You can roll up a brand new witch or warlock character, explore the dark forests and bogs of **The Gloaming**, join the tormented Knights of Saint Ydris, or test your mettle in *The Hideous Halls of Mugdulblub*!

The plan for future *Cursed Scrolls* is to continue with a theme for each so you can plug the whole zine right into your game world and instantly expand its boundaries.

**So, intrepid reader!** Youʼve dared to open this musty scroll and glance through its contents... Will you risk discovering what horrible hexes or magical boons await you after reading through the final word on the last page?

Fair warning: Youʼll probably be cursed. But thatʼs never stopped an adventurer worth their gold from reading a magical scroll anyway.

As the saying goes:

### Roll high and donʼt die!

-Kelsey Dionne, Dec. 2021
