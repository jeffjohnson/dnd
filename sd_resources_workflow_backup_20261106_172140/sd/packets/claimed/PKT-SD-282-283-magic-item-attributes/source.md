## MAGIC ITEM ATTRIBUTES {#p282}

### QUALITIES
A magic item can have some or all of the following qualities.

**Bonus.** Magic armor and weapons can have a bonus from +0 to +3. Armor with a bonus adds that amount to the AC it grants. A weapon with a bonus adds that amount to its attack and damage rolls.+0 items are the most frequent, and +3 items are very rare.

**Benefit.** A supernatural benefit gifted to the itemʼs wielder. Most magic items have at least one benefit.**Curse.** A negative effect bestowed on the itemʼs wielder. Some magic items have a curse.

**Personality.** A few magic items are conscious and have personalities that include virtues, flaws, a trait, and an alignment. Magic items with personalities can communicate telepathically with their wielders.

### CONSUMABLES

A creature must drink an entire potion or use an entire consumable item to gain its effects.

The user decides the order in which the effects take place on a consumable item.
